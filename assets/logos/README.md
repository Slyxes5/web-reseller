# Product logo assets

These marks identify the example products shown in the storefront prototype. Their names and marks remain the property of their respective owners; the page does not imply affiliation.

- `tstore.svg` is the original TStore mark created for this project.

- `spotify.svg`, `youtube.svg`, `gemini.svg`, and `applemusic.svg` are Simple Icons SVGs. Simple Icons describes its icon collection as CC0; brand marks remain trademarks of their owners.
- `canva.svg` is the Canva wordmark asset hosted on Canva's static asset domain.
